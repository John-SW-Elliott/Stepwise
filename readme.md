# StepWise — Privacy Policy

## Privacy Policy

StepWise is a diet, workout and body-tracking app that works entirely offline. This policy describes how your data is handled.

### 1. Data the app uses

To provide its features, the app uses personal and health-related information that you enter on your own device.

### 2. Where your data lives

All data is stored on your device only. The app has no server, creates no account and sends no data over the internet.

### 3. Sharing

No data is shared with the developer or with third parties. The app contains no advertising and no analytics services.

### 4. Backups and reports

You may, at your own initiative, export your data and share it through other apps on your device. Those files contain your data and remain under your control; StepWise does not receive or store them.

### 5. Permissions

The app requests only the permissions needed for the features you use — such as notifications for the reminders you set up, and file access when you choose to import or export your data. Permissions can be denied where your device allows it.

### 6. Deleting your data

You can erase all your data from within the app at any time. Uninstalling the app also removes your local data.

### 7. Children

The app is intended for adults.

### 8. Health notice

StepWise provides estimates and is not a substitute for medical, nutrition or physical-education professional advice.
