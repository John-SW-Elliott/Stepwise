# StepWise — Privacy Policy

---

## Privacy Policy

StepWise is a diet, workout and body-tracking app that works **entirely offline**. This policy describes how your data is handled.

### 1. Data the app uses

To calculate your diet, workouts and health indicators, you enter on your own device: sex, age, weight, height, activity level, body measurements (circumferences and skinfolds), body-fat percentage, meals, workout sessions, supplements and preferences (theme, language, alarms).

### 2. Where your data lives

All data is stored **on your device only**, in a local database (SQLite). The app **has no server, creates no account and sends no data over the internet** — it does not even request the internet permission.

### 3. Sharing

No data is shared with the developer or with third parties. The app contains no advertising and no analytics services.

### 4. Backups and reports

You may, at your own initiative, **export** a backup file (JSON) or a report (PDF) and share it through any app on your device. Those files contain your data and remain under your control; StepWise does not receive or store them.

### 5. Permissions

- **Notifications** (Android 13+): used only for the alarms you set up (meals, water, supplements, measurements and training). It can be denied without affecting anything else.
- **Run at startup**: reschedules your alarms after the device restarts.
- **File access**: only when you choose to import or export a backup.

### 6. Deleting your data

In *Settings → Erase all data* you remove everything immediately. Uninstalling the app also deletes the local database.

### 7. Children

The app is intended for adults. Its calculations use formulas for people aged 18 and over.

### 8. Health notice

StepWise provides estimates based on published scientific formulas and is **not a substitute** for medical, nutrition or physical-education professional advice.
